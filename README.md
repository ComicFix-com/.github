# ComicFix Open-Source Community Code of Conduct

**Purpose**  
ComicFix is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants, regardless of experience level, background, gender, race, or identity. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participants and steps for reporting unacceptable behavior.

**Our Standards**  
All community members are expected to:

1. **Be Respectful**  
   - Respect all community members regardless of their skill level, background, or personal identity.
   - Use welcoming and inclusive language. Avoid discriminatory, demeaning, or offensive remarks.

2. **Be Collaborative**  
   - Encourage and support fellow developers by providing constructive feedback.
   - Collaborate with kindness. Offer help when needed and respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

3. **Be Transparent**  
   - Share knowledge openly and foster a collaborative learning environment.
   - Attribute work and ideas properly. Give credit where it’s due.

4. **Be Responsible**  
   - Take responsibility for your actions and behavior within the community.
   - Contribute in ways that benefit others and the overall development of the community.

5. **Promote Inclusivity**  
   - Ensure that everyone feels safe and welcome, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, ability, race, or religious beliefs.
   - Respect cultural differences and value diverse opinions.

6. **Handle Disagreements Gracefully**  
   - Disagreements are inevitable, but they should be handled constructively and respectfully.
   - If disagreements arise, seek to understand the other person’s perspective and resolve conflicts in a peaceful, respectful manner.

**Unacceptable Behavior**  
The following behaviors are considered unacceptable in our community and will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment of any kind**, including offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, disability, or religion.
- **Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory remarks**.
- **Unwelcome sexual attention or advances**.
- **Trolling, spamming, or inflammatory comments** intended to provoke others.
- **Doxxing** (sharing private or personally identifiable information without consent).
- **Disruptive behavior** that hinders constructive discussions or collaborations.

**Reporting Violations**  
If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it immediately to our team at **[comicfixxx@gmail.com]**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

We encourage you to report:
- Threatening or inappropriate behavior.
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Violations of community standards.

**Consequences**  
Participants asked to stop any inappropriate behavior are expected to comply immediately. Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in the following actions:
- A warning.
- Temporary or permanent expulsion from the community without a refund (if applicable).
- Removal of contributions or posts related to the violation.

**Acknowledgment**  
By participating in ComicFix, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct and contribute to fostering a positive, inclusive community. Let’s work together to make ComicFix a welcoming and inspiring place for everyone.
